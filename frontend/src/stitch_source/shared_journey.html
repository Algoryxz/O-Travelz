Day 1
Bhubaneswar

Temples of Ekamra Kshetra

Begin your journey in the 'Temple City of India'. The ancient Ekamra Kshetra is home to hundreds of sandstone temples surrounding the sacred Bindusagar Lake. The architectural grandeur speaks volumes of the Kalinga dynasty's craftsmanship.

Lingaraj Temple

Morning Exploration
tour

A magnificent 11th-century temple dedicated to Harihara. Note the exquisite carvings on the vimana (main spire) which rises to 55 meters. Non-Hindus can view the complex from the specially erected viewing platform.

Mukteswara Temple

The 'Gem of Odisha Architecture'

restaurant

Curator's Dining Pick

For lunch, experience authentic Odia thali at Dalma. Don't miss the Dalma (lentil and vegetable stew) and Chhena Poda for dessert.

Day 2
Puri & Raghurajpur

Holy Shore & Artisan Heritage

Travel south to the coastal city of Puri. After receiving blessings at the mighty Jagannath Temple, we retreat inland to a village where every house is an art studio, keeping ancient traditions alive.

Coastal Calm

Chilika Environs

The brackish water lagoon provides a serene counterpoint to the bustling temple towns. A brief morning detour before heading to Puri.

palette

Raghurajpur Heritage Village

Spend the afternoon wandering through this idyllic village. Interact with master craftsmen creating Pattachitra (palm leaf scroll paintings) and intricate stone carvings directly on their front porches.

lightbulb Tip: Purchase directly from artisans to support the local economy.
Day 3
Konark

Sun Temple & Chandrabhaga Dawn

The climax of the triangle. Arrive before dawn to witness the sun rising over the Bay of Bengal, illuminating the colossal chariot temple of Surya, the Sun God.

camera UNESCO World Heritage

The Stone Chariot

Designed as a massive chariot with 24 carved wheels pulled by seven horses, the temple is a masterpiece of proportions and carving. Pay special attention to the wheels, which function as sundials capable of calculating time to the minute.
