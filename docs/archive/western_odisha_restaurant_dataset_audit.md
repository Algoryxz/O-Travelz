## Executive Summary

An evidence-backed, deduplicated restaurant and dining dataset expansion was conducted across all **11 districts of Western Odisha**. Data collection prioritized official restaurant portals (Mayfair Hotels, The Grand Siba, Magdalene's Cafe, Anshula Resorts, OTDC Panthanivas) and district administration tourism directories.
