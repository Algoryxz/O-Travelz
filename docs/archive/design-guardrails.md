# O-Travelz Design Guardrails (Permanent Rules)

`STATUS: VERIFIED`

These guardrails are binding for all future frontend iterations, agent contributions, and pull requests.

---

## 1. What NEVER to Reintroduce (Strict Anti-Patterns)

1. **NO AI Visual Clichés**:
   * NEVER use purple/cyan AI gradients, glowing neon orbs, or floating blurred blobs.
   * NEVER use sparkle icons as primary decorative elements.
   * NEVER brand core features with superficial "AI-Powered", "Ask our Chatbot", or "Magic AI" labels.
2. **NO "Glass UI" / Frosted Plastic**:
   * Avoid excessive backdrop-filter blurs, transparent cards, and frosted overlays.
   * Use solid, architectural material surfaces (`#111827`, `#0B1220`, `#FAF8F5`) with crisp hairline borders (`#263244`).
3. **NO Government Tourism Portal Aesthetic**:
   * Do NOT cover the UI with literal cultural stamps, intricate temple clipart, or saturated orange/yellow color schemes.
   * Keep the design globally appropriate: Konark, Chilika, or Koraput should look as high-end as Kyoto or the Amalfi Coast.
4. **NO Generic 3-Card SaaS Grid Spam**:
   * Do NOT render 12 identical cards in a row. Use asymmetric editorial compositions, coverflow carousels, full-bleed photography, and contextual detail blocks.
5. **NO Font Sprawl**:
   * Do NOT import more than the standard typography scale (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `DM Serif Display`, `DM Mono`, `Noto Sans Oriya`).

---

## 2. What ALWAYS to Prioritize

1. **Editorial Curation + Deterministic Precision**:
   * Always show real distances (`km`), verified operating hours, dynamic live weather, and estimated transit times.
2. **Whitespace as a Feature**:
   * Let photography breathe. Use generous padding, clear typography hierarchy, and deliberate vertical rhythm.
3. **Restrained Color Hierarchy**:
   * Earthy sandstone, terracotta, and deep coastal teal should guide traveler focus, not decorate every component.
4. **Mobile Ergonomics**:
   * Ensure touch targets are at least 44px, bottom-sheet flows are comfortable with one thumb, and keyboard inputs do not cause layout jumping.
5. **Accessibility & Contract Integrity**:
   * Maintain 4.5:1 minimum text contrast, visible focus rings (`outline: 2px solid #14B8A6`), semantic buttons, and full screen-reader accessibility.
