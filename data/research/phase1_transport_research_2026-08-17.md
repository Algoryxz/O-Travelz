# Phase 1 Transport Research

Official CRUT-hosted AMA E-Ride PDFs establish 13 routes, ordered stop names, route lengths, operating hours and approximate peak headways. The route-map PDF is the source for ER-02 through ER-07 and ER-13; the newer updated route document is retained for the routes it explicitly exposes. No coordinates are present in the inspected PDFs. Coordinates therefore remain unresolved.

AMA Bus official CRUT pages establish the operational route/network-map artifacts. A route 12 stop sequence was transcribed from a third-party page that states its data comes from an official PDF; route 09 schedule times were taken from a dated schedule transcription. These sources are explicitly marked secondary where applicable.

Current CRUT AMA E-Ride page states a flat Rs 10 fare. Odisha Transport states bus fares are automatically revised and current fare orders are issued; no static Mo Bus fare is asserted here without the current order contents.
